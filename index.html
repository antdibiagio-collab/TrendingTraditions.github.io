About Us

Trending Traditions was founded on the belief that meaningful connection should not be lost in an increasingly digital world. While technology has made communication faster than ever, many interactions have become temporary. Text messages disappear, notifications are forgotten, and social media posts quickly fade from memory. At the same time, young consumers are rediscovering physical media through vinyl records, film photography, books, and collectibles. Trending Traditions brings that same philosophy to greeting cards. We create products inspired by sports, music, entertainment, and the cultural moments that shape modern life while preserving the timeless emotional impact of card-giving.
